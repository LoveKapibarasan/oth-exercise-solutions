Exercise 6-1: Approximation of TSP
1. Create MST.
MST is the lower bound of TSP.
2. Create multigraph by duplicating edges of MST.
Now this round trip has around double cost than TSP tour.
3. Apply transitive(metrics) low.
If A  B  C, then A  C cost is smaller than A  B  C cost.
4. Now the graph is round-trip-able and has around double cost than TSP tour.
