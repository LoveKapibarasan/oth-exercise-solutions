TM that acceptsA— build a TM with the properties stated in the proof.
Hint:You can duplicate the alphabet (useΣ×Σinstead ofΣas the alphabet) and imagine the tape as
having two tracks, one for the part left of the first input symbol and one track for the rest.
Solution:
•No movement left of the initial input:Use a two-track tape (by expanding the alphabet to
pairs). Track 1 represents the tape from cell 0 extending right. Track 2 represents the tape
from cell -1 extending left (folded over). The TM simulates moving left of cell 0 by switching
to Track 2 and reversing its movement direction.
•Unique accepting configuration (clean tape, head at 0, states 1, timep(|x|)):We add a new
unique accepting states clean. Whenever the original TM accepts, we transition tosclean. In this
state, the TM sweeps across the tape to erase all symbols, moves the head back to position 0,
writes a ’1’, and then stays ins clean doing nothing (looping) until exactlyp(|x|)steps have
passed.
•No path stops ifx/∈A:Modify all previously halting, rejecting states (or undefined transi-
tions) to enter a ”sink” states reject. Ins reject, the TM simply loops forever.
•Looping instructions 11→s 110:As described above, the new accepting states clean gets
exactly this instruction to ”burn” remaining time up top(|x|)steps.
