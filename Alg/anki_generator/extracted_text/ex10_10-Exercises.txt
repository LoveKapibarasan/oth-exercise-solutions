We use structural induction on the game tree.
•Base Case: No further recursive calls are made.The structural induction is rooted in two base
cases where the score is evaluated directly without recursion:
1. Draw (c=0):The checkdepth == 0triggers, returningv=0. By definition,S(N) =0.

depth(i.e.,c). By definition,S(N) =c.
