\begin{problem}[Reduction from 3SAT to CLIQUE]

Consider the following Boolean formula in 3-CNF:

\[
\varphi
=
(x_1 \lor \neg x_2 \lor x_3)
\land
(\neg x_1 \lor x_2 \lor x_4)
\land
(x_1 \lor x_3 \lor \neg x_4).
\]

Using the standard polynomial-time reduction from \textsc{3SAT} to \textsc{CLIQUE}:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Construct the graph \(G_\varphi\) corresponding to \(\varphi\).
    
    \item Clearly specify the vertices and edges of \(G_\varphi\).
    
    \item Determine whether \(G_\varphi\) contains a clique of size \(3\).
    
    \item If such a clique exists, give the corresponding satisfying assignment for \(\varphi\).
    
    \item Briefly explain why, in general,
    \[
    \varphi \in \textsc{3SAT}
    \iff
    \langle G_\varphi, m \rangle \in \textsc{CLIQUE},
    \]
    where \(m\) is the number of clauses in \(\varphi\).
\end{enumerate}

\end{problem}
