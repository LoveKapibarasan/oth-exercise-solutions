b) Primary keys and Foreign keys

Primärschlüssel und Fremdschlüssel

  • branch(branch_name, branch_city, assets)
  • customer(ID, customer_name, customer_street, customer_city)
  • loan(loan_number, branch_name, amount)
    • Foreign key loan.branch_name references branch.branch_name.
  • borrower(ID, loan_number)
    • Foreign key borrower.ID references customer.ID.
    • Foreign key borrower.loan_number references loan.loan_number.
  • account(account_number, branch_name, balance)
    • Foreign key account.branch_name references branch.branch_name
  • depositor(ID, account_number)
    • Foreign key depositor.ID references customer.ID
