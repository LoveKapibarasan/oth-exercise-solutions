


\subsection*{Binomial Distribution}
\[
E[X] = np
\]

\[
\mathrm{Var}(X) = np(1-p)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\sigma = \sqrt{np(1-p)}.
\]


These follow from the fact
1. a binomial random variable can be expressed as the sum( Linearity ) of $n$ independent Bernoulli trials, each with mean $p$ and variance $p(1-p)$.
